About Us
Prime Location & Accessibility

Rajnandini Banquet is conveniently located in the heart of Pune—just 10 minutes from Pune International Airport and 15 minutes from Pune Railway Station. Whether your guests are local or traveling from out of town, they’ll appreciate the ease of access to our venue.

Customizable Venue & Packages

Our banquet can comfortably host 800 to 1,000 guests, making it ideal for weddings, receptions, corporate functions, and more. We offer tailored menus and personalized planning to suit your tastes. Choose between per-person pricing or opt for our all-inclusive event packages—flexibility to match your needs and budget.

Unique, Memorable Celebrations

Why settle for ordinary? At Rajnandini Banquet, your event will stand out. Our venue offers both large and intimate spaces, creating an experience your guests will remember—not just another event they've attended before. With a relaxed, stylish, and welcoming atmosphere, you can truly enjoy your special day surrounded by family and friends.
